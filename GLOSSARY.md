# Glossary: DRAM Capacitor Mold Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**α (asymmetry factor):** Effective fraction of the full capillary pressure that is unbalanced across a pillar, averaged over the span. Reference median 0.15 for an orderly drying front; approaches 1 at droplets and secondary fronts. (Ch. 8.1, 10.4)

**ACL (amorphous carbon layer):** Hard mask for the support-open etch; reference 300 nm with a 25 nm SiON cap. (Ch. 2.5, 3.4)

**Adsorbed layer:** Thin film of water or alcohol on the surface that catalyses vapor-HF etching; must stay below condensation. (Ch. 4.6, 7.1)

**Array edge:** Boundary between the free-standing pillar lattice and the embedded periphery; site of undercut and peeling. (Ch. 11.4)

---

## B

**Bifluoride (HF₂⁻):** The more reactive fluoride species in HF solution; raised by NH₄F buffering. (Ch. 4.1)

**Bottom stop:** 20 nm LPCVD-class SiN at the base of the mold; the HF barrier above the landing-pad level. (Ch. 2.3.4, 12.4)

**BPSG:** Boron-phosphorus silicate glass; the lower oxide of the mold. Etches ≈ 600 nm/min in 5% HF. (Ch. 2.2)

**Bridge:** Residual oxide or residue holding two pillars together or at a fixed small gap. (Ch. 12.3.2)

---

## C

**Capillary condensation:** Condensation of vapor in a narrow gap below bulk saturation (Kelvin effect). (Ch. 7.2, 16.1)

**Cluster (defect):** Group of failed or leaning cells larger than a pair; often beyond the repair resource. (Ch. 16.3.2)

**Collar:** The ring of support or stop nitride around a pillar; its tightness sets the clamp and the HF seal at the pillar foot. (Ch. 11.1.2, 12.4.3)

**Collapse margin (M):** Ratio of the collapse threshold to the linear deflection at the reference asymmetry; pair margin M = (g/8)/δ₀. Reference 2.76 with IPA. (Ch. 10.3)

**Column:** The hole cut by the support-open etch through the top support, upper oxide, and middle support. (Ch. 3.1)

**Crescent:** The part of a pillar top exposed inside a support-open opening; 15 nm deep, 317 nm² at reference overlay. (Ch. 2.4, 3.3)

---

## D

**Damköhler number (Da):** Ratio of reaction speed to diffusion speed; ≈ 7 × 10⁻⁶ for the lower-oxide path. (Ch. 4.4)

**Dip-out:** Removal of the mold oxide in HF through the support openings. Reference 5 wt% HF, 25 °C, 105 s. (Ch. 4, 6)

**Dummy pillars:** Inactive pillars outside the array that support the support ledge at the array edge. (Ch. 11.4.3)

---

## E

**Elastocapillary collapse:** Snap-through of pillars into contact when capillary force, rising as the gap closes, exceeds elastic restoring force. (Ch. 10.3)

**EOT:** Equivalent oxide thickness of the capacitor dielectric; reference 0.50 nm. (Ch. 1.2)

---

## F

**Flower defect:** Ring of leaning pillars around a central point where a droplet dried or a particle pinned the meniscus. (Ch. 9.3, App. G.3)

**Front (drying):** The moving boundary between wetted and dry forest during drying. (Ch. 8.3)

---

## G

**g (gap):** Distance between neighbouring pillar surfaces; 17 nm average, 13 nm top, 11.5 nm at the bow. (Ch. 1.3)

**Guard ring:** Continuous wall around the array that stops lateral HF undercut into the periphery. (Ch. 11.4.3)

---

## H

**H_eff:** Exposed pillar height after supports are subtracted; 1410 nm reference. (Ch. 1.2.2)

**HFE solvent:** Hydrofluoroether-type low-surface-tension solvent (γ ≈ 14 mN/m). (Ch. 8.1)

**HV-SEM:** High-voltage SEM that images through the top support; checks middle-support opening and leaning. (Ch. 15.1–15.2)

---

## I

**IPA displacement:** Replacement of rinse water by isopropyl alcohol before drying. (Ch. 8.2)

---

## L

**LAND step:** Final support-open step that lands 50–150 nm into BPSG. (Ch. 3.5)

**Late leaning:** Leaning that occurs during the dielectric or plate deposition after a successful dry. (Ch. 16.2)

**Leaning:** Lateral displacement of a pillar from vertical, usually into contact with a neighbour. Specification ≤ 1 per 10⁹ pillars. (Ch. 10)

**Ligament:** Narrow strip of support nitride between holes in the lattice; site of stress concentration. (Ch. 11.2)

**Lower span:** Free length of pillar between the bottom stop and the middle support; 760 nm, the weakest span. (Ch. 1.3.3, 10.1)

---

## M

**Marangoni drying:** Drying using a surface-tension gradient from IPA vapor dissolving into the meniscus. (Ch. 8.4)

**Middle support:** 50 nm SiN at 770–820 nm depth that halves the pillar's free length. (Ch. 2.3)

**Mold:** The oxide and nitride stack into which capacitor holes are etched and which is then removed. (Ch. 1, 2)

---

## O

**Opening cell:** The hexagonal area served by one support-open opening; 7015 nm², 4.05 pillars. (Ch. 2.4, App. E.12)

**Overetch (dip-out):** HF time beyond the longest normal path; reference 39 s over 66 s. (Ch. 12.5)

---

## P

**Pair threshold:** Linear deflection at which two neighbours pulled toward each other become unstable; g/8 = 2.1 nm. (Ch. 10.3.2)

**Pillar:** Solid TiN bottom electrode filling the capacitor hole; 32/28/24 nm, 1.6 µm tall. (Ch. 1.2, 2.1)

**Plate:** Top electrode and fill (TiN + SiGe or W) that encloses the forest. (Ch. 16.1)

**Pre-wet:** Short DIW or dilute rinse before HF to fill the columns and avoid trapped gas. (Ch. 4.5.2)

---

## Q

**Queue time:** Time between dry and dielectric ALD; reference limit 4 h in air, 12 h in N₂. (Ch. 13.2.3, 16.1)

---

## R

**Residual oxide:** Oxide remaining on pillars or supports after the dip-out; specified as not detectable. (Ch. 12)

---

## S

**Seam:** Line or plane in the pillar centre where the TiN growth fronts met; can open in the support open. (Ch. 2.1, 13.4)

**Sequential removal:** Two-stage mold removal with two support opens, dips, and dries. (Ch. 14.2)

**SN1 / SN2:** Support-open steps through the top and middle nitride supports. (Ch. 3.2.3)

**Solid fraction:** Fraction of a support that remains nitride after the support open; 39% top, 46% middle. (Ch. 2.3.3)

**Supercritical CO₂ drying:** Drying above 31.0 °C and 7.38 MPa where no liquid–gas interface exists. (Ch. 8.6)

**Support lattice:** The perforated top and middle nitride supports that hold the pillars. (Ch. 2.3, 11)

**Support open (SO):** Plasma etch that cuts openings through the supports and upper oxide. (Ch. 3, 5)

---

## T

**Top loss:** TiN removed from pillar crescents in the support open; ≤ 5 nm specified. (Ch. 3.3, 13.3)

**Top support:** 120 nm SiN at the top of the mold. (Ch. 2.3)

**Trapped gas:** Air left in a column that the liquid fails to enter; leaves oxide under it. (Ch. 4.5)

---

## U

**Undercut (array edge):** Lateral HF etch of periphery oxide beyond the last opening; ≈ 400 nm in BPSG. (Ch. 11.4.2)

**Upper span:** Free length between the middle and top supports; 650 nm. (Ch. 10.1)

---

## V

**Vapor HF:** Gas-phase oxide removal with HF and an alcohol catalyst; no meniscus, phosphorus residue from BPSG. (Ch. 4.6, 7)

---

## W

**Watermark:** Residue and leaning ring left by a droplet drying on the forest. (Ch. 9.4)

**Wet-to-wet transition:** Overlapping fluid changes so the wafer never dries between steps. (Ch. 6.1.3)
